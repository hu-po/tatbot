# Ink palette

The installed palette is merged palette v9 (`cad/merged-palette-v9`), one
printed 180 × 178 × 6 mm piece for the merged rig. It carries seventeen ink
caps, a touch-trigger probe, a Raspberry Pi camera aimed at the probe ball, the
drawing e-stop and an AprilTag on a flat seat. It is the only palette. It is
independent of the robot URDF; its pose in the arm base is measured after every
placement.

| Source | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `urdf/palette.urdf` | The palette body and its fixed frames. Generated by the CAD build; do not edit |
| `urdf/meshes/palette/merged_palette_v9.stl`, `.glb` | Body mesh (visual and collision, decimated to 40,000 faces) and the viewer's copy |
| `config/palette.yaml` | The seventeen slots, named after the URDF `inkcap_*` links, and cap sizes |
| `config/palette_geometry.json` | Revision `merged_palette_v9`, the URDF and body frame, the viewer mesh, the synthetic simulation pose, measured rim overrides (`rim_z_m`, none yet) |
| `config/fiducials.json` | `targets.palette`: AprilTag 36h11 ID 0, 46 mm black square |
| `config/palette_load.yaml` | Recorded cap contents |

## Frames

`palette_root` is on the probe axis 55.6 mm under the ball's centre, where
v11's was, so the probe and camera frames are v11's exactly and their
calibrations carry over (`runtime` in `cad/merged-palette-v9/design.json`). +X
points from the probe toward the Pi camera, +Y rearward, and +Z is up. The
base's top face is at Z −7 and its underside at Z −13.

| Frame | Position (mm) | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `palette_tag` | 114.5, −111.5, −7 | The sticker's seat centre on the base top; its turn measured (below) |
| `palette_probe_ball` | 0, 0, 55.6 | Centre of the probe's 2 mm ball |
| `palette_camera` | 122, 0, 55.6 | The Pi camera lens; its +X points at the ball |
| `palette_estop` | 128, −51, 37 | The upward-facing e-stop's panel; its +X points up |

The `inkcap_*` frames are cap **pocket floors**, not rims. A rim is its floor
plus the cap's outside height from `config/palette.yaml`, which puts the large
caps' rims 7 mm and the medium and small caps' 6 mm above `palette_root`. An
entry in `rim_z_m` overrides the computed rim for that cap once it is measured.

`config/palette.yaml` states each cap by its **outside**: `diameter_m` across
the rim, `height_m` from rim top to cap bottom, and `wall_m` for the rim, wall
and base. Outside, the caps are 15 × 14, 11 × 10 and 8 × 10 mm (diameter ×
height; large, medium, small), in pockets of 15.6, 13.2 and 9.6 mm. The
outside is what rests in the pocket and sets the rim height; the bore a tool
enters is derived (`ink_spec.CapSize.bore_diameter_m`, `.depth_m`) and is a
millimetre narrower.
Every cap serves the arm that draws, the one `stack.yaml` `arms` drives; each
placement still needs reach and clearance checks.

## The palette tag

The same AprilTag 36h11 ID 0 sticker, 46 mm black edge in a 58.75 mm white
square, sits flat on its 60 mm seat centred at (114.5, −111.5) mm on the base
top.

The printed pattern's turn on the seat is measured: the `palette_tag` frame's
+X runs along the palette's +X (`runtime.installed_tag` in the design,
`tag_pose_status="confirmed"` in the URDF). The overhead D555 found it as on v11
(2026-09-30): of the seat's four quarter turns, only that one put the e-stop, the
camera plate and the first large cap where its depth found them (2026-11-08).
The probe's thin stem does not resolve in the D555's depth from the overhead
mount. The station measures the palette only from a confirmed tag frame.

The previous v10 tag transform and palette placement are retired; no old
camera measurement is transplanted to the new print.

## Measuring the palette pose

The probe calibration measures the station afresh in every run, never from a
stored pose: `tatbot ros station` places it in the arm's base from the
overhead D555's view of the palette tag, through the arm's registration
(`ros/README.md` §7). The arm registration and palette placement must describe the current rig.

`tatbot ros setup` measures the drawing arm's station, as does:

```sh
tatbot ros station
```

The ROS stack measures the station afresh from the D555, validates the views,
and carries the full `palette_tag` transform from `urdf/palette.urdf` into the
arm base. The subsequent calibration run enforces freshness and checks for
station movement. The earlier camera-scan placement (`tatbot vision palette`
and its viewer preview) is retired. A missing placement never falls back to
the synthetic simulation pose.

## Dipping

The needle cartridges dip. A dip aims by the station touch, the probe's ball where the arm's own
touches found it, and the caps' CAD offsets about it, not by the overhead
fix alone (`ros/README.md` section 6).

## The probe and the camera

The touch probe and the Pi camera are for tool-tip and station calibration at
the ROS 2 stack's palette station (`ros/README.md` section 7). The probe is
normally closed, wired to the palette Pi's GPIO27. The camera is an Arducam
64 MP autofocus module (OmniVision OV64A40) on the palette Pi's CSI
connector, aimed along −X at the probe ball 122 mm away. This mount now reaches the documented near-focus distance;
focus and image scale must be checked on the installed camera. Measurements
from the former 55 mm mount do not describe the new optical setup.
The probe relay and the driver's probe guard read the probe, and the probe
calibration touches the tool's tube end on its ball (`ros/README.md` §7). The
camera watches the tip contact-free: `tatbot ros calib sweep` from joint-6
turns, and `tatbot ros calib height` for the ball's height that dips aim by.

## Retired

The eight-cap rack and the three-tip volcano fixture (`cad/palette-v2`) are
retired, with their meshes and their 81 mm ID 22 tag, and so is everything
built on the fixture's three pits: the planted-tip refinement of the palette
pose, cup teaching, and the per-arm hand-guided recipe with its kinematic
refinement. None of their commands or calibration phases exists now.
`cad/palette-v2` remains only because the integrated-station concept
(`cad/integrated-station-v1`) reads its geometry. The v11 touch probe replaces
the pits for tool-tip and station calibration.
